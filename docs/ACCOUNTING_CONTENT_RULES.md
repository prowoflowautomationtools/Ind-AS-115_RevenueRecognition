# Accounting Content Rules

Ind AS 115 content must be treated carefully because the correct accounting depends on the requirements and the facts and circumstances of each contract.

## Source and review

1. Use the latest notified version of Ind AS 115 and applicable amendments as the primary reference.
2. Check relevant appendices, implementation guidance and other applicable standards where needed.
3. Record paragraph references supporting substantive accounting explanations.
4. Have technical content reviewed by a suitably qualified accounting reviewer before treating it as approved learning material.
5. Distinguish the wording of the standard from explanatory examples, assumptions and practical observations.

## Explain judgement areas

For examples and decision tools, make assumptions visible. Avoid presenting a single answer as automatic where analysis depends on identifying the contract and performance obligations, determining and allocating the transaction price, variable consideration and its constraint, significant financing components, contract modifications, over-time versus point-in-time recognition, measuring progress, principal-versus-agent considerations, contract costs or disclosures.

This is a reminder of important topics, not a complete checklist.

## Calculator limitations

The current SSP calculator demonstrates basic allocation using relative stand-alone selling prices. Do not imply that it resolves all allocation cases. Discounts, variable consideration and other specific allocation requirements may need separate analysis under the standard.

## Educational disclaimer

The app supports learning and practice. It does not replace the notified standard, professional advice, engagement-specific analysis or review of the actual contract and evidence. Users should verify calculations and conclusions independently.
